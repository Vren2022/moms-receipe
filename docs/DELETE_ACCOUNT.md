# Nuskha — Delete your account

You can delete your Nuskha account and all of its data at any time.

## Option 1: In the app (instant)
1. Open **Nuskha** and tap the **account icon** (top right of the home screen).
2. Tap **Delete account**, then **Yes, delete everything**.

Your account is deleted immediately.

## Option 2: By email (if you no longer have the app)
Send an email to **virenvaviya2022@gmail.com**
- Subject: **Delete my Nuskha account**
- From the email address you used for Nuskha (or tell us that address in the message).

We delete the account within **7 days** and reply to confirm.

## What gets deleted
- Your account: email address, name and password (or your Google sign-in link)
- All your recipes, including the original text you entered, who taught them, favorites and "next time" notes
- Your settings (preferred language, last opened time) and the record of your AI recipe requests

Nothing is kept after deletion, and deleted data cannot be recovered.
