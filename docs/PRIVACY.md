# Nuskha — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 September 2026_

Nuskha saves your family recipes and turns them into step-by-step cooking guides. We keep as little about you as we can.

## What we store
- **Your account:** email address, the name you typed at sign-up (or your name from Google if you log in with Google), and your password in encrypted (hashed) form. We never see your Google password.
- **Your recipes:** the text you paste or type, the recipe we make from it, who taught it to you, favorites, notes and when you last cooked it.
- **App settings:** your preferred recipe language and when you last opened the app.
- **Usage count:** a timestamp each time you ask the AI to make a recipe, so we can keep a fair daily limit.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising IDs. We do not show ads and we do not sell or share your data for marketing.

## Who processes it
- **Supabase** stores your account and recipes (database and login).
- **OpenRouter / Google Gemini** receive the recipe text you submit, only to turn it into a structured recipe. Don't include personal details in recipe text that you don't want processed.
- **Google** handles "Continue with Google" if you choose it. We receive only your name and email address from Google.
- **Gmail** delivers the sign-up and login code emails.

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## Who can see your recipes
Only you. Every recipe is tied to your account and protected so other users cannot read it.

## Deleting your data
Open **Account → Delete account** in the app. Your account, recipes and settings are deleted immediately and cannot be recovered.
No longer have the app? See [Delete your account](DELETE_ACCOUNT.md) to request deletion by email.

## Children
Nuskha is not directed at children under 13.
